Reading the numbers

Pump distributes a fixed number of pop locations across 25 positions. At Medium, three positions are pops. Before the first pump, the pop risk is 3/25 = 12%.

If your first pump survives, there are now three pops among 24 remaining positions. Next-pump pop risk is 3/24 = 12.5%.

A 1.11× payout does not mean a 100% chance of winning. Every extra pump increases the conditional risk, and the game's stated 2% house edge remains.
